Distribution provenance
  The bundled Latin WOFF2 files were copied unchanged from the owner's
  Sketchcoded site. On 2026-09-30, all seven files were compared byte-for-byte
  with the corresponding package/files entries in these Fontsource releases:
    https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/dm-sans/v/5.3.0
    https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/caveat/v/5.3.0
    https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/newsreader/v/5.3.0
  All seven matched. The complete licence notices were obtained from the
  authoritative Google Fonts repository on the same date.

No font file was modified for sciencewithagents. No additional font families or
weights are included. The website serves these files locally; it does not request
fonts from Google Fonts, Fontsource or another third party at runtime.
