A private agent HQ on your own computer. Talk to Codex and Claude project managers in one place and follow the workers they bring in. Runs on your computer.

Projects, conversations and history stay on it. There is no sciencewithagents account: agents work through the Codex and Claude sign-ins you already have, and the app never asks for your password. Phone access is optional.

What it does

  • One place for project chats

    Codex and Claude managers side by side. Each conversation keeps its own provider, model, native tools and history.

  • QUARK paces the work

    It reads your reported allowance and your computer's load, queues work by priority and pauses a project near the budget you set. Shares are estimates, not a bill.

  • Managers and their workers

    A manager splits the job into bounded tasks for researchers, builders and independent reviewers. By default, the manager applies work once it passes review. For any project, you can choose to check and approve changes yourself first.

  • Decisions stay on the record

    Assignments, conversations, tool results, reviews and decisions are saved and searchable, linked back to the original evidence.

  • Your browser and your phone

    Open the app in a desktop browser. Optionally pair a phone over a private connection, with a passkey and a confirmation on your computer.

  • Keep working in VS Code

    With the optional companion (preview), share a live Codex or Claude editor chat into the same conversation list without moving it.
